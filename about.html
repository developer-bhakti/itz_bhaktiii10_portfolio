Who I am

Bhakti Purnale

Content Creator / Digital Creator

Hi, I'm Bhakti — a content creator and digital creator who loves making lifestyle, fashion and short-form content. For me it all starts with a simple idea: turn an outfit, a place or an everyday moment into something people actually enjoy watching.

I create reels and YouTube Shorts, share exploring and get-ready-with-me content, and work on photoshoots and creator-style UGC. Whether I'm in front of the camera or behind it, I care about story, colour and the little details that make a frame feel alive.

I'm open to collaborating with brands and creatives on reels, UGC, photoshoots and social media content.

Content that feels natural rather than overly commercial.
